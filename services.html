Our Expertise

Comprehensive Environmental & Facility Solutions

Facility Cleaning & Management

Professional cleaning and maintenance for offices, hospitals, and public spaces, ensuring hygiene and compliance with health standards.

  • Daily, weekly, and deep cleaning
  • Disinfection & fumigation
  • Janitorial staff training
  • Facility audits & compliance checks
  • HSE-compliant operations

Serving INEC, hospitals, corporate offices, and public buildings across Nigeria.

Municipal Waste Management

Efficient collection, disposal, and recycling of municipal waste, promoting sustainable urban environments.

  • Door-to-door collection
  • Waste sorting & recycling
  • Landfill management
  • Public awareness campaigns
  • Compliance with AEPB & NESREA

Partnered with local governments for cleaner, greener cities.

Oil & Gas Waste Management

Specialized handling and remediation of oil and gas waste, ensuring environmental safety and regulatory compliance.

  • Hazardous waste containment
  • Oil spill cleanup & bioremediation
  • Drilling waste disposal
  • Site restoration
  • DPR & FMEnv compliance

Major contractor to HYPREP for Ogoniland cleanup.

Environmental Consultancy

Expert audits, EIAs, and sustainability strategies to support compliance and environmental stewardship.

  • Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA)
  • Environmental Audits
  • ESMP & ARAP development
  • Policy & regulatory advisory
  • Stakeholder engagement

Accredited by FMEnv. Trusted by World Bank, AfDB, and HYPREP.

Waste Audits & Compliance

Comprehensive waste audits to optimize processes, reduce costs, and ensure regulatory adherence.

  • Waste stream analysis
  • Cost reduction strategies
  • Compliance reporting
  • Training & documentation
  • Continuous improvement plans

Helping clients save up to 30% on waste management costs.

Sustainability Advocacy
