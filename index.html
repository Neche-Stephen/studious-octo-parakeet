Pinnacle Health & Environmental Services Ltd is an implementation company active in Environmental Management, Environmental Health and Facility Management. Formed in August 2006 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 1990, with RC 661920, to address environmental protection and maintenance culture. Starting with 12 staff, we now have over 350 employees across Nigeria, managing +60 facilities with +N200Million in assets. We provide practical, individualized services, combining local content with international quality standards.
