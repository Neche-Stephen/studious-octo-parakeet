Sustainable Cleaning & Waste Management: Creating Healthier Spaces for All

Healthy, well-managed environments protect people and improve productivity. At Pinnacle Health & Environmental Services Ltd, we combine science-led cleaning, compliant waste management and practical sustainability to keep facilities safe—across offices, healthcare, industrial sites and public spaces in Nigeria.

“If it’s not safe for people and the environment, it’s not Pinnacle standard.”

Pinnacle Operations HSE Lead Quality • Safety • Integrity

1) Facility Cleaning That Prioritizes Health

Our teams use risk-based cleaning plans, validated disinfectants and color-coded tools to prevent cross-contamination. We align with client SOPs and regulatory guidance while minimizing harsh chemical exposure wherever possible.

2) Smarter Waste Management for Municipal & Oil & Gas

From route planning and segregation programs to hydrocarbon-impacted soil handling, we build systems that reduce risk and cost. For oil & gas, our specialists support spill response, remediation and environmental monitoring—backed by delivery experience under HYPREP.

3) Compliance & Continuous Improvement

We help clients meet environmental obligations with audits, data logging and corrective action tracking. Our approach integrates training, signage, and simple behavior nudges that keep programs effective long after launch.

4) Practical Steps You Can Start Now

  • Implement color-coded cleaning to eliminate cross-use of mops and cloths.
  • Introduce a three-stream waste system (recyclables, organics, residual).
  • Schedule periodic waste audits to pinpoint savings opportunities.
  • Adopt safer-use chemical guidelines and improve storeroom labelling.
