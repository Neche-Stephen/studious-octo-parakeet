Who We Are

Government-trusted environmental, health & facility solutions

Pinnacle Health & Environmental Services Ltd was incorporated on July 31, 2006 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 1990 with RC 661920.

Starting with 12 staff, we now employ over 350 professionals across 36 liaison offices in Nigeria, managing 60+ facilities with over N200 million in assets.

We provide practical, individualized services combining local content with international quality standards — serving government, oil & gas, and private sectors.
